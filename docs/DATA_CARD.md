## 3. What this data does not cover

This section is scored, and it is the main risk to real-world use.

- **No real prices.** Every price is synthetic. National series (WFP, FAOSTAT) are not the same thing as a local buyer quote for one grade, form and county. A pilot needs a dated feed from a co-op or market partner who allows its use.
- **Four Kenyan counties, two coffee forms, two grades.** The records cover nothing outside Nyeri, Kiambu, Murang'a and Kirinyaga, cherry or parchment, and grades A or B (and only some combinations). Everything else abstains by design.
- **No real voices.** Speech accuracy has only been checked on synthetic speech (TTS played back into Whisper). There are no recordings of farmers, women and men, older speakers, background noise, or 8 kHz phone audio from Kenyan networks.
- **Accent mismatch.** The only offline Swahili voice is Congolese Swahili (`sw_CD`), not Kenyan Swahili.
- **The main local language is missing.** Gĩkũyũ (Kikuyu), the main language around Nyeri, is not supported by Whisper, Piper or the LLMs used here.
- **Templated language.** The fine-tuning sentences come from 12 templates, so they don't capture code-switching (for example Sheng, or Swahili-English mixing), spoken numbers ("mia moja na tano"), dialect words or messy speech.
- **Business Q&A is English-only training data.** Hindi, Swahili, Chinese and Korean answers rely on the base model, which is weaker in Swahili.
- **No crop-health data.** Disease questions are refused and referred to a person. Nothing here is trained on crop images (studio datasets underperform on field photos anyway).

## 4. Named local language and a less-supported one

**Named language:** **Swahili** (Kenya). It works end to end offline: a Swahili question gets a Swahili answer with the record's price and date. Hindi, Chinese and Korean are also supported for testing breadth.

**How the tool fares in a less-supported language.** Swahili is itself the less-supported case among the five:

| Stage | Swahili today | Fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Speech-to-text | Whisper language ID was correct in our round-trip test, but words were garbled ("Nyeri" → "Konyeri", "parchment" → "parishmenti"); `large-v3-turbo` was clearly better but ~3× slower | Hotwords for place and product names; ask for one missing field at a time |
| Understanding | Rules catch digits, "shilingi", "daraja A", district names. Spoken numbers ("mia moja na tano") are not converted, so the price is dropped rather than guessed | Answer with the reference price only; ask for the number |
| Business answers | Gemma 3 4B answers are understandable but grammatically weak | Approved Swahili templates for every price outcome |
| Voice | Local Congolese-Swahili Piper voice; ElevenLabs model used here does not support Swahili | Text shown on screen; human referral |

**Gĩkũyũ, a truly low-resource language.** None of the current models support it. The path is Meta MMS (speech recognition and text-to-speech for over 1,000 languages, listed in the brief; confirm its Gĩkũyũ coverage first), fine-tuned on consented Common Voice-style recordings collected with the co-op. Until then, Gĩkũyũ callers are offered Swahili, English or a person.
